Policies

Please review before placing your order.

Order Processing

Processing time does not include shipping/transit time. Processing times may be extended during high-volume periods like prom, graduation, holidays, and sales. Please place time-sensitive orders well in advance.

Custom Designs

Colors may appear slightly different due to lighting, photography, and screen settings. Nail'd by Elise will make reasonable efforts to match your selected design and colors.

Changes and Cancellations

Review all order details before submitting. Changes or cancellations should be made as soon as possible. After 24 hours, production begins and changes may result in an additional charge.

Shipping Address

You are responsible for providing an accurate shipping address. Contact Nail'd by Elise immediately if you notice an error. Additional shipping costs from incorrect addresses are the customer's responsibility.

Shipping and Delivery

Tracking information will be provided when available. Carrier delivery dates are estimates and may be affected by weather, holidays, or shipping volume. Order early for events like prom, graduation, or birthdays.

Lost, Delayed, or Missing Packages

If a package is marked delivered but not received, contact the shipping carrier. If tracking shows a delay or stops updating, contact both the carrier and Nail'd by Elise.

Returned Packages

If an order is returned due to an incorrect customer-provided address or unsuccessful delivery, the customer may be required to pay re-shipping costs. If the return is due to a Nail'd by Elise error, no additional charge applies.

Damaged Orders

Contact Nail'd by Elise within 24 to 48 hours with your name, order info, clear photos of damaged nails, photos of damaged packaging, and a description of the issue. Eligible issues will be reviewed and resolved appropriately. Damage from improper application, removal, storage, or use is not covered.

Returns and Refunds

Contact Nail'd by Elise if an order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially different from what was ordered. Refunds, replacements, or other resolutions will be determined on a case-by-case basis.

Customer Communication

Please be respectful when contacting Nail'd by Elise. Harassment, threats, fraudulent claims, or abusive behavior will result in refusal of future service.
